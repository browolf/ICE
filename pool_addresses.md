# Explanation of Pools 
(text originally from https://ice.io/coin-economics - Note ION chain Addresses)

## Community Pool 

The community allocation represents 28% of the total ION token supply, equivalent to 5,842,127,776.35 ION, distributed to community members based on mining activity and participation during the initial phase of the project. This allocation was designed to reward early supporters who contributed to the growth and security of the network before mainnet launch.  Community tokens are not subject to a lock period and were made immediately available upon distribution. These tokens can be freely held, transferred, staked, or used in governance processes, enabling active participation in the ecosystem from day one. This structure ensures broad token distribution, early liquidity, and strong community ownership within the Ice Open Network.

## Rewards pool 

The rewards pool represents 27% of the total ION token supply, equivalent to 5,790,667,813.05 ION, allocated to incentivize participation across the Ice Open Network. This pool is used to reward validators, node operators, creators, and other contributors who support network activity, security, and ecosystem growth. 
Tokens in the rewards pool are subject to a five-year lock period, held at the following address on Ice Open Network Chain: EQD6NGWja-1_e1QRL5To1L6O_iLr25pl-7EZ_YlvogcUkmoY.

The tokens are released on a quarterly basis, starting from mainnet launch, with each release unlocking a proportional amount. This vesting structure ensures long-term incentive sustainability while preventing sudden supply increases, aligning rewards distribution with continued ecosystem participation.

## Team Pool 

The team allocation represents 25% of the total ION token supply, equivalent to 5,287,634,358.82 ION, reserved to incentivize and reward the ongoing contributions of the core team responsible for building, maintaining, and expanding the Ice Open Network.

These tokens are subject to a five-year lock period and are held at the following address on Ice Open Network Chain: EQDR2MSm7NVTn0A9k_qSqiYnHQ4wrHC6fFL6jPYLR7rfQOdo.

Tokens are released on a quarterly basis, starting from mainnet launch, with each release unlocking a proportional amount. This vesting structure is designed to ensure long-term commitment from the team, align incentives with the network’s success, and support continuous development, operations, and ecosystem expansion over time.

## Treasury Pool 

The treasury pool represents 10% of the total ION token supply, equivalent to 2,115,053,743.53 ION, allocated to support strategic and operational needs across the Ice Open Network. This pool is designed to provide flexibility for initiatives that strengthen the ecosystem’s stability, visibility, and long-term sustainability.

Treasury tokens are subject to a five-year lock period and are held at the following address on Ice Open Network Chain: EQBp_r5Urgi1hPK6cH6VBEDCpKya9XgEaXc56UsUA761At9k.

Tokens are released on a quarterly basis, starting from the BNB Smart Chain distribution date, with each release unlocking a proportional amount. The treasury pool is used for activities such as liquidity provision, exchange partnerships, market-making support, and other strategic initiatives aligned with the growth and resilience of the network.

## Ecosystem Growth and Innovation Pool 

The ecosystem growth and innovation pool represents 10% of the total ION token supply, equivalent to 2,115,053,743.53 ION, dedicated to expanding and strengthening the Ice Open Network ecosystem. This allocation supports initiatives that drive adoption, innovation, and long-term ecosystem development.

Tokens in this pool are subject to a five-year lock period and are held at the following address on Ice Open Network Chain: EQBBNVB44slw0rveWQZsY4zFG9tlqeBR_XyLWxuiR95GIbth.

The tokens are released on a quarterly basis, starting from the BNB Smart Chain distribution date, with each release unlocking a proportional amount. The pool is used to support partnerships, onboarding of new projects, third-party development and marketing services, and other initiatives that contribute to the growth, diversity, and resilience of the ecosystem.
